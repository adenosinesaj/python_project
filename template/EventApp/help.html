Frequently Asked Questions

What is ManageHoise? and how does it work?

ManageHoise? is a Django-based two-user platform connecting event Vendors and Customers. Vendors can showcase products and services (catering, decorations, rentals), while Customers can browse offerings, manage carts, and organize event needs.

How do I register as a Vendor or Customer?

Visit the SignUp page, fill in your details, and select your designated role (Vendor or Customer). Your profile (`C_profile`) will automatically be configured according to your role.

As a Vendor, how do I add or manage my products?

Once logged in as a Vendor, go to the Product Section to add new products with photos, descriptions, and pricing. You can also edit or remove your active product listings anytime.

How do I add items to my Cart and Checkout?

Browse the product catalog or specific vendor portfolios, click "Add to Cart", and review your selected items on the Cart page before proceeding to final Checkout.

How can I view my order history?

Navigate to your account menu and click on My Orders to view past purchases and completed checkout logs.

How do I contact vendor management or support?

You can visit the Vendors List page to view individual vendor profile details or reach out directly to our ManageHoise? team for general site assistance.
